Delete your account

LetsMeetVerse · Last updated: November 11, 2026

中文版在下方

You can delete your LetsMeetVerse game account, and the data that goes with it, whenever you like. The same account is used by the web game, the desktop app and the iOS and Android apps, so deleting it once deletes it everywhere.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Sign in, then open Settings (the gear on your phone).
  2. Under Account, choose Delete account…
  3. Type your character's name to confirm, and choose Delete my account forever.

Your account is deleted at once and you are signed out on every device. This cannot be undone.

If you can't sign in

Email noahyu0315@gmail.com from the address on your account (or the one you pre-registered with), with the subject "Delete my LetsMeetVerse account", and tell us your username or character name. We may ask one question to make sure the account is yours, then delete it within 30 days and write back when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in: username, password hash, sign-in tokens, and the link to Google (or Apple) if you used one.
  • Your email address and your pre-registration, if you made one.
  • Your character, profile, progress, belongings, in-game coins, home and pets.
  • Your private messages, your lines in the city chat history, photos you are in, your paintings, and pictures you uploaded (for example to a billboard).
  • Reports you made and reports about you, your friend list, blocks and mutes, and every link other players had to you (friends, invites, relationships).
  • Shops, houses and lots you own go back on the in-game market.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. A painting another player bought from you stays theirs, without your name. Our hosting provider's ordinary server logs (which can include IP addresses) and its database backups expire on the provider's own schedule.

To remove only your pre-registration (no game account), use the same email address above.

删除账号

LetsMeetVerse · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 11 日

你可以随时删除自己的 LetsMeetVerse 游戏账号及相关数据。网页版、电脑版和 iOS / Android App 用的是同一个账号，删除一次即全部删除。

在游戏里删除（最快）

  1. 登录后打开设置（手机里的齿轮）。
  2. 在账号下选择删除账号…
  3. 输入你的角色名确认，然后点永久删除我的账号。

账号会立即删除，所有设备都会退出登录。删除后无法恢复。

无法登录时

请用账号绑定的邮箱（或预约时用的邮箱）发信到 noahyu0315@gmail.com，标题写“删除我的 LetsMeetVerse 账号”，并告诉我们你的用户名或角色名。我们可能会问一个问题来确认账号属于你，然后在 30 天内删除，完成后回信告知。

会删除哪些内容

  • 你的登录信息：用户名、密码哈希、登录令牌，以及绑定的 Google（或 Apple）账号。
  • 你的邮箱和预约记录（如有）。
  • 你的角色、资料、进度、物品、游戏币、住处和宠物。
  • 你的私信、城市聊天记录里你说的话、有你在的照片、你的画作，以及你上传的图片（例如广告牌）。
  • 你提交的举报和针对你的举报、好友列表、屏蔽和静音名单，以及其他玩家与你之间的所有关联（好友、邀请、关系）。
  • 你名下的店铺、房子和地块会重新回到游戏内的市场。

会保留哪些内容

不会保留任何能识别你的信息。其他玩家从你那里买下的画作仍归他们所有，但不再显示你的名字。托管服务商的普通服务器日志（可能包含 IP 地址）和数据库备份按服务商自身的周期过期删除。

如果只想删除预约记录（没有游戏账号），也请发信到上面的邮箱。
