Hi, I'm Noah

I'm a designer. I've never made a game before; I just really love playing them. So I taught myself to build with AI, and started making the world I always wanted to live in.

LetsMeetVerse is an online life sim set in real LA, where you live a cozy second life next to real people. Meet friends, get a job, move into your first studio, buy property, invest, open your own shop, and one day get married.

With every release I write down here what changed. Thanks for following along.
