Make the city yours

Days in LA run on your schedule: work when you need coins, shop when you have them, and stop to chat on the way.

  • Move in

    Pick a neighborhood, a floor plan and a floor. The concierge has your keys ready.

  • Make friends

    Say hi on the street, chat, add friends and message them from your in-game phone.

  • Work a shift

    Pull espresso, run the register or cook the line. Earn coins, learn skills and get promoted.

  • Decorate

    Pick a floor plan, then fill it with sofas, lamps and plants, and try on new looks at the wardrobe.

Pick your neighborhood

Your starter studio can be in any of these four. The map also takes in Mid-City and Venice, and you can get around by bike, bus or car.

  • Santa Monica

    Beach days, the pier and an ocean breeze.

  • Hollywood

    Star-set sidewalks, old theaters and bright marquees.

  • Koreatown

    Late-night food, karaoke and cafés that stay busy.

  • Downtown LA

    Skyline views from loft-style towers.
