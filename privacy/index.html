Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

中文版在下方

LetsMeetVerse is an independent online life-sim game, currently in Early Access. This page says what information the game and this website keep about you, why, and how to have it removed.

What we keep

  • Pre-registration. Your email address, the language you were reading in, whether you asked for news, and the date. If you pre-register with Google, we also keep your Google account ID and the fact that Google has verified the address.
  • Game account. Your username and character name; your password, stored only as a salted hash that we cannot read back; and your email address if you add one.
  • Google sign-in. If you sign in with Google, Google tells us your Google account ID, your email address and your first name. We never see your Google password, and we ask for no other Google data (no contacts, no files, no calendar).
  • What you do in the game. Your character, progress, belongings and in-game money; in-game photos and pictures you upload (for example to a billboard); and the chat and direct messages you send, which are stored so they can be delivered and so abuse can be dealt with.
  • Technical data. Your IP address is used briefly to limit repeated attempts and keep the service safe, and our hosting provider keeps ordinary server logs. Our website counter uses no cookies. It hashes network addresses with a fresh daily salt to estimate daily uniques. The counter saves daily totals for 90 days. It discards the salt and address hashes after each day and never saves raw addresses.
  • Reports and blocks. If you report a player, we keep the report, who made it and the reported player's public chat from the half hour before, so a moderator can review it. Blocks and mutes are kept with your account.
  • On your device. The game stores a sign-in token and your settings (such as language and sound) in your browser, the desktop app or the mobile app. We use no advertising cookies.

What we use it for

  • To run the game and keep your account and progress.
  • To email you when the game opens, and to send news only if you asked for it.
  • To prevent cheating, spam and harassment.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

Only the services the game runs on: Railway (game server and database), GitHub Pages (this website) and Google (only if you choose Google sign-in). We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Removing your information

You can delete your game account and its data yourself, at any time: in the game, open Settings → Delete account. It happens at once and cannot be undone. If you can't sign in any more, see Delete your account, or email noahyu0315@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we will remove your pre-registration, or delete your game account and its data. You can also ask what we hold about you, or have it corrected.

Children

LetsMeetVerse is not directed to children under 13, and they should not create an account or pre-register. Some features are for adults only.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 23 日

LetsMeetVerse 是一款独立开发的在线生活模拟游戏，目前处于抢先体验阶段。本页说明游戏和官网会保存哪些关于你的信息、为什么保存，以及如何删除。

我们保存什么

  • 预约。你的邮箱、当时使用的语言、是否愿意接收新闻，以及预约日期。如果用 Google 预约，还会保存你的 Google 账号 ID，以及该邮箱已由 Google 验证这一事实。
  • 游戏账号。用户名和角色名；密码只以加盐哈希的形式保存，我们无法还原；以及你自己填写的邮箱（可选）。
  • Google 登录。如果你用 Google 登录，Google 会告诉我们你的 Google 账号 ID、邮箱和名字。我们看不到你的 Google 密码，也不会索取其他 Google 数据（没有通讯录、文件或日历）。
  • 你在游戏里做的事。角色、进度、物品和游戏币；游戏内拍的照片和你上传的图片（例如广告牌）；以及你发送的聊天和私信，保存它们是为了送达消息和处理滥用行为。
  • 技术数据。IP 地址会被短暂用于限制重复尝试、保护服务安全；托管服务商会保留普通的服务器日志。官网访问计数器不使用 Cookie。它用每天新生成的随机盐对网络地址做哈希处理，估算每日独立访问量。计数器保留 90 天的每日汇总，每日结束后删除随机盐和地址哈希，从不保存原始地址。
  • 举报和屏蔽。如果你举报了某位玩家，我们会保存这条举报、举报人，以及被举报玩家在此前半小时内的公开聊天，供管理员审核。屏蔽和静音名单随你的账号保存。
  • 你的设备上。游戏会在浏览器、电脑版或手机 App 里保存登录令牌和你的设置（如语言、声音）。我们不使用广告 Cookie。

用来做什么

  • 运行游戏，保存你的账号和进度。
  • 在游戏开放时发邮件通知你；只有你同意时才发送新闻。
  • 防止作弊、垃圾信息和骚扰。

我们不出售你的信息，也不把它用于广告。

还有谁会接触到

只有游戏运行所依赖的服务：Railway（游戏服务器和数据库）、GitHub Pages（官网）和 Google（仅当你选择 Google 登录时）。法律要求时我们也可能披露信息。

删除你的信息

你可以随时自己删除游戏账号及其数据：在游戏里打开 设置 → 删除账号，立即生效，无法恢复。如果已经无法登录，请看 删除账号 页面，或用你注册时的邮箱发信到 noahyu0315@gmail.com，我们会删除你的预约记录，或删除你的游戏账号及其数据。你也可以询问我们保存了哪些信息，或要求更正。

儿童

LetsMeetVerse 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，他们不应注册账号或预约。部分功能仅限成年人使用。

变更

本政策如有变更，新版本会连同新的日期发布在这里。
